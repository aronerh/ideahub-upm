# Whole app redesign: Graphite Glow

Scope: every page of the app (home, idea page, publish form, sign-up, sign-in, error and empty states).
Mode: Operate. Audience: UPM students publishing first, investors browsing second.
Constraint: the structure of the forms, sign-up, sign-in and the ideas list stays the same.
Approved mocks: .impeccable/mocks/decision/gallery-b.html.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, modern product that lets students' ideas carry the page. It refuses both the bright playful student look and the flat grey directory.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white or near-black neutrals, graphite ink, one green. Geist throughout at two weights. Soft green light sits behind the page top. White cards with hairline borders, letter badges, 8 to 12px radii.

STORY: A visitor sees real ideas at once, learns anyone can read and publishing needs a UPM email, and reaches Publish from every page in one click. A student finishes five fields and lands on a page confirming the idea is live.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with logo, Explore ideas and the green Publish button at top right. Large headline, one line of context, the UPM rule, then the two-column list, newest first. Signature move: a card lifts with a green edge and its arrow slides in on hover and keyboard focus.

FORM: Graphite, the category standard held to Linear and Vercel, with life added through light and motion. Fourth on the ordered list. seed:36895dc6d4120987

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
